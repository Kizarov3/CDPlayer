FIRST LAUNCH

  1. Open the .dmg and drag CDPlayer into Applications. One file for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
  2. Open CDPlayer. If macOS says it can't check it, click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway near the bottom, then confirm with your password. Only the first time: the app isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate. On macOS 14 and older, right-click → Open → Open is enough.
  3. Pick your music folder. Your albums go onto the shelf, and a short guide shows you round the window.

Keep it in Applications: from there it updates itself.

  1. Download the .exe and put it in a folder of its own (not inside a .zip). There's nothing to install.
  2. Double-click it. If Windows SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click More info → Run anyway. Only the first time: the app isn't signed with a paid certificate.
  3. Pick your music folder. Your albums go onto the shelf, and a short guide shows you round the window.

Windows 10 and 11. It updates itself where it is.

  1. Make the AppImage executable, in a terminal or in the file's Properties:
    chmod +x CDPlayer-*-linux.AppImage
  2. Double-click it. There's nothing to install.
  3. Pick your music folder. Your albums go onto the shelf, and a short guide shows you round the window.

The AppImage updates itself, and AppImageUpdate, Gear Lever and appimaged can update it too. It's in the AppImage catalog.

Updates: when a new version is out, an AVAILABLE button lights up in the player's top-left corner. One click downloads it (checked against its SHA-256), then RESTART TO UPDATE, and your queue is kept.

PLAYER LOOK

WHAT'S IN IT

THE CD SHELF

Every album in your music folder, spine-out, kept up to date as you add or rip albums. Sort by artist, year, colour or most played, or put up dividers of your own. Albums you never play gather dust, new ones arrive shrink-wrapped, and PULL ONE pulls out an album at random, dusty ones first.

THE BOOKLET

Each album's tracklist, lyrics and credits, marked in pen as you listen: tally marks for each song, your favourite circled, and on the back cover how many times you've played the whole album.

LYRICS & KARAOKE

Lyrics are found online and light up word by word as they're sung. On English songs, small voice models that come with the app time each word to the singing in your file, offline. ABC spells out Japanese, Chinese, Korean and Cyrillic lyrics in Latin letters.

PHONE REMOTE

Scan a QR code and your phone becomes the remote, in its browser, on your Wi-Fi. There's nothing to install and nothing goes over the internet. Skip, set the volume, pick albums from the shelf, rearrange the queue.

REAL CDS

Play an audio CD, named from MusicBrainz, or RIP it to verified, tagged FLAC in one click.

STREAMING

YouTube Music and SoundCloud play like your own discs. Your Spotify albums and playlists play too, with Premium.

CD CHANGER

Five discs in a carousel, each keeping its own music. DISC SKIP, and keys 1 to 5 to play a slot.

THE SOUND

Gapless albums, AUTOMIX, a 10-band EQ, Sound Check, spatial audio for headphones, lossless up to 192 kHz.

MAKE IT YOURS

Eleven themes and seven player looks, from a hi-fi deck to a terminal. Make your own theme and share it as a code.

HISTORY

What you played, day by day. Import your plays from Spotify, Last.fm or Apple Music, and scrobble to Last.fm and ListenBrainz.

NOW PLAYING CARDS

A picture or a video of what's playing, with lines of its lyrics, ready to send. Discord status too.

EVERY FORMAT

MP3, M4A/ALAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, OGG and Opus play out of the box. English and Russian.
